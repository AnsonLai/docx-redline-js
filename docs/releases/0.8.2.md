## @ansonlai/docx-redline-js v0.8.2

Version 0.8.2 fixes two edits that changed more than they were asked to. A localized replacement at the edge of a
hyperlink could pull the plain text beside the link into it, and Reject All could reorder text around a manual line
break after a localized replacement. Both were wrong only in the tracked-change markup: the accepted view of issue 3
and the rejected view of issue 4 did not match the source document.

**Upgrade if you replace text inside or beside hyperlinks, or in paragraphs that contain manual line breaks.** Nothing
else changes.

---

### Compatibility and Migration

- **CLI contract remains version 8.** No response shapes, flags, error codes or capabilities were added, removed or
  changed.
- **No API or schema changes.** Operations, options and declarations are unchanged.
- **Behavior change callers may notice:** the tracked changes for the edits below are now smaller and leave unchanged
  text as unchanged. The same edit no longer deletes and reinserts text that did not change, so a document redlined
  with 0.8.1 and the same edit redlined with 0.8.2 can differ in their revision markup, though not in the intended
  result.

---

### 1. Fixed: Replacement Absorbs Adjacent Plain Punctuation Into a Hyperlink

[Issue #3](https://github.com/AnsonLai/docx-redline-js/issues/3). A paragraph held a hyperlink showing `example.org`
followed by a plain `.` run. Replacing `example.org` with `example.net`, through localized `replacements` or a full
`modified` paragraph, produced a hyperlink that showed `example.net.` after Accept All. The full stop had moved into
the link.

The reconstruction-mode word diff treated `example.org.` as one token that spanned the hyperlink boundary. It deleted
the whole token and inserted `example.net.` inside the link, even though the `.` did not change.

- Where a replacement's shared prefix or suffix reaches a wrapper boundary inside the deleted text, that text is now
  kept unchanged instead of being deleted and reinserted.
- A boundary at the edge of the whole change does not split it, so extending text inside a link (`link` to
  `hyperlink`) still stays inside the link.
- The hyperlink keeps its relationship, and the adjacent `.` stays in its own plain run.

---

### 2. Fixed: Reject All Reorders Text Around a Manual Line Break

[Issue #4](https://github.com/AnsonLai/docx-redline-js/issues/4). A paragraph contained `\ttabbed<w:br/>Line with `.
Replacing `tabbed` with `aligned` and then rejecting all changes gave `\ttabbedLine\n with `. The deleted text had moved
across the line break.

Two causes combined:

- The diff glued the `<w:br/>` placeholder into the word tokens on either side of it, so a change beside the break
  swallowed text across it.
- The writer emits a reference placeholder that is shared by a deletion and an insertion only with the insertion,
  after all of the deleted text.

The fixes:

- Manual line breaks and note-reference placeholders are standalone tokens in the word diff. The tokenizer takes a new
  internal `atomicChars` option for this, on `computeWordDiffs` and `wordsToChars`.
- A change group that contains a reference on both sides is split at that reference, so the reference stays an
  unchanged anchor between its own before and after edits.

Reject All now restores `\ttabbed` and `Line with ` on either side of the break, in the original order.

### 3. Testing

- **New suite:** `tests/reconstruction_boundary_fidelity_tests.mjs` covers both issues, in localized and full-paragraph
